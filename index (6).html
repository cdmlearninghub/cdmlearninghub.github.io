Questions

Frequently asked

I'm not from pharmacy. Can I join?

Yes. People from life sciences, nursing, biotech and other science backgrounds work in CDM. The sessions start from the basics.

Do I need programming knowledge?

No. Entry-level CDM roles need attention to detail, clear written English and basic Excel. No coding is needed for this programme.

Will I get a certificate?

No. This is an independent learning programme and no certificate is issued.

Does this guarantee a job?

No. The programme helps you understand CDM and prepare for interviews, but it does not offer placement or guarantee employment.

How are the live sessions held?

Online, once a week for four weeks. Timings are shared when you sign up.

What's the difference between the plans?

CDM Notes is self-study material. Interview Prep is a 1:1 session focused on fresher interviews. The 4-Week Live Course is taught live once a week. The Complete Bundle includes the live course, the notes and a 1:1 mock interview.

How do I get the e-book, query kit or practice pack?

Choose the resource in the sign-up form and pay on Razorpay. Your files are emailed to you within 24 hours. They are for your personal use only.

I live outside India. Can I join?

Yes. Enter your WhatsApp number with your country code. If card payment from your country doesn't work, email us and we'll share another way to pay.

How do I pay?

Fill in the sign-up form and click Continue to payment. You'll get a button that opens Razorpay's secure page, where you can pay by UPI, card or net banking.

What is the refund policy?

CDM Notes and digital resources are not refundable once they have been emailed. See the Refund Policy for details.
